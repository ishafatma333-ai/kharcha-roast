# 🔥 KharchaRoast

> *"Bhai ₹3,430 sirf Zomato pe uda diya is hafte, aur 4 din mein rent due hai!"*

**KharchaRoast** is an interactive Hinglish AI personal finance & UPI expense tracker prototype (inspired by Cleo, built for India). It tracks your monthly salary, protects your rent money, and roasts your impulse spending in real time.

*(Built as my 2nd project on GitHub!)*

## ✨ Features
- **3 Hinglish Roast Modes:** Switch between *Pyaar Se* (polite), *Mild Roast* (sarcastic), and *Full Savage* (no mercy).
- **Live UPI Payment Simulator:** Test how a Zomato, Swiggy, or Myntra UPI debit triggers an instant roast notification.
- **Rent Shield & Safe-to-Spend:** Automatically locks your rent amount and shows what you can actually spend today.
- **Conversational AI Chat:** Ask *"Can I afford dinner tonight?"* or *"Myntra sale dekhun?"* and get data-backed Hinglish answers.
- **Spending Breakdown:** Visual category chart powered by Chart.js.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **Frontend:** HTML5, Tailwind CSS, Vanilla JavaScript
- **Charts & Icons:** Chart.js, Lucide Icons
- **Backend Logic (Sample):** Python (UPI SMS parser & threshold-based roast engine)
